## Answers to the usual worries

**"What if the internet goes down?"**
Be careful here. As of today the POS keeps its catalogue available offline, but full offline billing with later sync is planned and not shipped (see docs/market-plan.md, M3). Do not promise it in print until it ships. Suggested wording until then: "Billing works over any broadband or mobile connection."

**"Who owns my data?"**
The shop does. [Confirm export policy: customers, products, invoices exported on request or self-service.]

**"Can I leave?"**
[Confirm: export of all data in CSV/Excel at any time, no lock-in fee.]

**"Do I need a special machine?"**
No. A phone, tablet or computer with a browser is enough. Counters that want a touch terminal, printer, scanner and cash drawer can buy a kit. [Confirm whether srikit sells kits; market range for a touch terminal is ₹33,000 – ₹77,000, printer ₹7,000 – ₹8,500, cash drawer about ₹5,850.]
