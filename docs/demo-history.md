# Demo trading history

`pnpm --filter @rbms/api seed:history <slug> [--days 90] [--profile <name>] [--dry-run]`

Makes a demo shop look as if it has traded for N days: bills, payments, receipts, credit notes, till sessions, purchases,
expenses and customers, all created through the **real kit services** (`apps/api/src/kit.ts`), so GST reports, stock, the
credit book and the dashboard agree with each other. Code: `apps/api/src/demo-history/`, CLI `apps/api/src/seed-history.ts`.

## Rules

- **Dates**: every record is dated inside the last N days (IST) and never after now. The kit takes an optional clock
  (`createKit(env, log, { now })`); only this script passes one, so bills' `issuedAt`, stock movements, batch movements,
  till open/close times, dining tickets and expense dates carry the simulated time. Columns that default to the database's
  `now()` (`created_at` on bills, payments, customers) are today's real time: views sorted by those show "today".
- **Deterministic**: every day is planned from the seed `slug:date`, every bill from `slug:bill:date:index`. Same slug and
  data give the same history; a longer window gives the same days.
- **Top-up / idempotent**: a day with a till session of the profile's registers is done and skipped. A rerun fills only the
  missing days up to today. Today is generated up to the current time and its current shift's drawer left open (like a
  live shop); the next run counts and closes it first. Issued bills can never be deleted, so there is no "undo": start
  from a fresh shop to regenerate.
- **Nothing is sent**: the script blanks the SMS / email / WhatsApp / push provider credentials for its own process
  (console drivers log only) and uses an in-memory Redis. Customer phones are `+91 9999 00xxxx` (the repo's fake test range).
- Invoice quota is not consumed (`skipInvoiceQuota`), so history does not eat the current month's plan allowance.
- Stock: items are stock-tracked (or untracked for menus and services), the opening stock is bought on the first day, and
  purchases are made whenever an item falls to its reorder level; sales never take stock below zero.
- Staff names are labels (till `openedByName` / `closedByName`, salary expenses); they are not sign-in users.

## Profiles (`profiles.ts`, pure data)

`kirana`, `pharmacy`, `restaurant`, `jewellery` (full), `textiles`, `hardware`, `services` (simple), `spa` (appointments + memberships), `repair` (job cards), `electronics` (serial / IMEI units), `tailoring` (garment job cards), plus `cloud_kitchen`, `tea_stall` and `spa`. Each defines bills per
weekday, closed days, opening hours with peaks, basket size, Pareto item popularity, payment mix, customers (named share,
credit customers, limits, repayment habits), returns rate, registers, till float, staff, monthly and weekly expenses,
suppliers and the catalogue. Missing catalogue items are created by name; items the shop already has are used as they are
(their prices kept), the rest of the catalogue added. Festival effects are in `calendar.ts` (`FESTIVALS`, scaled by each
profile's `festivalAffinity`): Pongal, Tamil New Year, Akshaya Tritiya, Vinayagar Chathurthi, Navaratri / Ayudha Pooja
(2026: 11 to 19 Oct), Deepavali (8 Nov 2026, 14-day build-up), Dhanteras, Karthigai Deepam.

## Industry hooks (`hooks/`)

- **kirana**: loose goods weighed (gram precision on shops with scales, PLUs created for loose items), salary-week
  credit-book settlements.
- **pharmacy**: medicines created with schedule and composition; stock arrives as batches with expiry (a few short-dated);
  sales take from batches first-expiry-first through the kit's invoice hook; H / H1 / X sales go to a saved customer and get
  a prescription (H1 register); the supplier bill is booked without stock lines so goods are not counted twice.
  Store #22, **Thunai Pharmacy & Wellness** (`thunai`, replaces the earlier `medplus`; picked automatically for the slug, pass `--profile pharmacy` anyway): the profile catalogue is the store's catalogue (91 items, `demo-stores/thunai-products.ts` reads it, so names match and nothing is created twice); the team on the tills and in salaries is the shop's real staff (manager = pharmacist in charge, cashiers, stock keeper; `prepare` reads the staff module); two registers, `Counter 1` / `Counter 2`, like the POS terminals. Run `seed:stores thunai`, invite the team (`/v1/staff/invites`) and run `src/seed-thunai.ts` for their names, then `seed:history thunai --profile pharmacy --dry-run`, then for real (about 40 s per simulated day). A 90-day window cannot make 61-90 / 90+ day dues, so clinics and nursing homes on credit with dated part-paid bills, and older / expired batches for the expiry report, are added afterwards through the API.
- **restaurant**: bills come from dining orders: dine-in on tables with KOTs in rounds, tickets served, sometimes a split
  bill; takeaway and delivery orders; lunch and dinner peaks. Falls back to counter billing if dining is off.
- **restaurant** profile catalogue (`demo-history/profile-restaurant.ts`): built from the Arusuvai Unavagam menu (`demo-stores/arusuvai-menu.ts`, 60 dishes, 86 portions), so bills sell the dishes the store shows; credit accounts (offices, hospitals, colleges, caterers) order in bulk for pickup or delivery. Run `seed:stores arusuvai`, then `seed:history arusuvai --profile restaurant` (the slug also defaults to it). Staff phones +91 90001 10101-10106.
- **jewellery**: daily gold and silver rates for each date, tagged pieces with weights, wastage, making and stone charges
  and HUIDs priced from the day's rate, topped up with a supplier bill; sales of one or two pieces; old-gold exchanges
  applied as payments.
- **jobcards** (profile `repair`, e.g. QuickFix): every bill is a job card delivered that day. The job is one of the shop's
  repair recipes (phone screen, battery with its part, SSD upgrade with a serial unit, AC service, washing machine bearing…),
  opened up to a few days earlier and moved received → diagnosed → awaiting approval → approved (online on the tracking page,
  or recorded from a call / WhatsApp / the counter) → in progress → trial → ready with dated timeline events (the job card
  service takes the simulated clock), parts and labour lines, an advance on bigger jobs, then billed (credit customers' bills
  go on the book with a due date) and delivered. Each day also opens a few jobs that are not delivered: cancelled ones
  (estimate declined), free warranty re-repairs, and in the last two weeks open jobs in every status so the job board looks
  live. The shop's real staff (names from the staff module) are used on tills and as technicians. Needs the `jobcards`
  feature (serial parts need `serials`). Run the demo store seed first (`seed:stores quickfix`), then
  `pnpm exec tsx --env-file-if-exists=.env src/seed-quickfix.ts` for the staff names, then `seed:history quickfix`.
- **electronics** (profile `electronics`, TechZone): the catalogue is built from the demo store's own products (`demo-stores/techzone.ts`, one entry per variant, named "<product> – <options>" as the kit names variants), phones with two IMEIs and big appliances with a prefixed serial are serial-tracked (`serial` on the catalogue entry): the hook gives the items their warranty months, receives a unit for every piece in stock (IMEIs with a correct Luhn check digit), receives more units whenever the engine restocks, puts one unit's IMEI / serial on each phone or appliance line (the kit marks it sold and starts its warranty from the bill date; a line with no unit left is dropped) and on about one day in five notes a warranty claim on a unit sold a week or more ago (the serials service has no simulated clock: a claim's time stamp is the real time of the run, sale and warranty dates are the bill's). Tills are the two POS terminals (`Counter 1`, `Counter 2`). Run `seed:stores techzone`, create the team (`apps/api/src/seed-techzone.ts` names the invited staff), then `seed:history techzone --profile electronics` (the slug also defaults to this profile). Business customers have electronics-trade names; credit customers' bills are part-paid or unpaid on 15 / 30-day terms, so the credit ageing fills up to 61–90 days within a 90-day window; older EMI-style dues (90+) are dated bills created through the invoices API.
- **tailoring** (store #20, Thaiyal Tailors & Boutique, Madurai; profile `tailoring`, picked automatically for the `thaiyal` slug, always pass `--profile tailoring` anyway): the same `jobcards` hook with the **garment** template (`hooks/jobcards-garments.ts`, chosen by `ctx.profile.id === "tailoring"`; repair behaviour is untouched). Every bill is a job delivered that day: blouses (simple, princess cut, designer, bridal with aari work), salwars, kurtis, pavadai and frocks, men's shirts and pants, alterations, falls and pico, and school uniforms in bulk. Measurements in inches are generated per garment type with the template's own keys (one body size drives bust, waist and hip so they agree), the trial date is the day before delivery for garments with a fitting, bridal / aari / suit / uniform jobs are quoted and approved first, jobs open 0 to 21 days earlier and move received, measured (diagnosed), approved, in progress, trial, ready with dated events, an advance on jobs of ₹800 and up (30 to 50%), and a free alteration within 7 days now and then (zero-priced job). Bulk uniform orders are billed to the school credit customers (business names with a GSTIN, 30-day terms, `onCredit` 0.85), their quantity follows the size slab; a school never brings a single alteration. Open jobs are spread across every status in the last two weeks (more in flight than a repair bench, since a blouse takes days). Staff names on tills and job cards are the shop's real team (`seed-thaiyal.ts` names the invited users). Catalogue: built from the demo store's own services, one entry per variant (`profile-tailoring.ts`), 5% GST, SAC 998821, untracked. Run `seed:stores thaiyal`, invite the team, `pnpm exec tsx --env-file-if-exists=.env src/seed-thaiyal.ts`, `seed:history thaiyal --profile tailoring --dry-run` (confirm it prints `profile tailoring`), then without `--dry-run`. A 90-day window cannot make 61-90 / 90+ day dues, so the showcase adds dated part-paid school bills through the invoices API afterwards.
- textiles, hardware, services: no hook (standard billing).
- **tea_stall** (store #19, Theneer Kadai; `--profile tea_stall`, picked automatically for the `theneer` slug): counter-first, no hook (standard counter bills, no tables or KOTs): 285 to 360 small bills a day (a tea, a vada, two cups), morning and 4 to 7 pm peaks, cash and UPI, two registers (morning / evening counter), office tea-card customers on credit, and daily milk / fruit / snack-ingredient expenses (`weeklyExpenses` entries with `weekday: "daily"`). The menu is the store's own (`demo-stores/theneer-menu.ts`). About 2 minutes of runtime per simulated day. A 90-day window cannot make 61-90 / 90+ day dues, so the showcase adds dated part-paid office bills through the API afterwards.
- **bookings** (profile `spa`, store #21 Sukham Ayur Spa; `--profile spa`, picked automatically for the `sukham` slug): every bill is an appointment made through the bookings service on the shop's day calendar (the first free therapist who does the treatment, start worked back from the bill time, 60 to 150 minutes), moved booked → confirmed → arrived → in service, billed from the appointment (it becomes completed) with the front desk's till, and paid by the engine (UPI, card, cash). About 9 to 17 a day. Each day also books a few appointments that end cancelled (with a reason) or no-show, and on the last day appointments for the coming week (booked / confirmed) plus one guest arrived and one in service. Membership plans of the shop are sold to new members (about one every two days, billed at the desk at one of the day's bill times), renewed by about 6 in 10 members within five days of ending, and active members check in through the days (more often when sessions run low). The hook is stateless (members, plans and calendar are read back), so a top-up run continues the same shop. An appointment that finds no free slot within two and a half hours falls back to a counter bill. The kit passes the simulated clock to the bookings and memberships services (`createKit(..., { now })`). Needs the demo store's catalogue (`seed:stores sukham`, which also sets up the team, bookable services, therapists' hours and the three plans). The corporate wellness tie-ups on credit with ageing in every bucket (61-90 and 90+ days) are added afterwards by `pnpm exec tsx --env-file-if-exists=.env src/seed-spa.ts` (dated, part-paid bank receipts). Always pass `--profile spa` and run `--dry-run` first: the slug map and the SV app default would pick `services` otherwise.
- **textiles catalogue** (no hook): ~55 items with HSN, barcodes and the apparel price-band rule. An entry with `band` / `barcode` that the shop lacks is created as a catalogue product (`kit.catalogue.service.createProduct`, with `gstRule` APPAREL_FOOTWEAR_GST_RULE for Ch. 61-63), the engine carries `gstRule` onto bill lines, so the GST summary shows both the 5% and 18% bands. Fabric, sarees and yarn (Ch. 50-59) stay at 5%. `customers.businessNames` sets trade-specific business-customer names. A 90-day window cannot produce 61-90 / 90+ day dues (terms are 15-30 days), so showcase shops add dated part-paid credit bills through the API afterwards.
